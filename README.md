Psychological Effects of COVID-19: Comprehensive Behavioral Analysis.

📊 Project Overview.

This data science project conducts a rigorous behavioral and demographic evaluation of the psychological and operational impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic on individuals globally. By processing a multidimensional dataset of 1,175 participants, the analysis isolates how shifting environments, occupational pressures, and remote contexts altered daily performance, sleep consistency, and skill acquisition across distinct demographic groups.

💡 Key Data Insights Captured.

Demographic & Core Age Profiling: A targeted assessment mapping participant age brackets, highlighting a high-density focus on the emerging workforce and student populations aged 19–25 (representing 32.6% of the study).

Occupational Layout Analysis: An evaluation of population density across professional roles, identifying "Working Professionals" and "Students in College" as the primary cohorts steering the baseline data.

Environmental Shift vs. Mental Relaxation Matrix: A granular cross-tabulation heatmap quantifying how physical working environments (Home, Office, Mixed) directly correlated with changes in subjective relaxation levels, exposing systemic burnout windows.

Pre-Pandemic Operational Baselines: A historical tracking analysis mapping average working hours across careers to contextualize the severity of the subsequent pandemic-induced lifestyle disruptions.

Gender-Segregated Skill & Sleep Trends: Advanced multivariate bar charts isolating behavioral variations between genders, tracking both adaptive resilience (new skill acquisition scales) and physiological stress indicators (sleep quality fluctuations).

🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated.

Advanced Data Engineering: Python (Pandas, NumPy) — performing multi-variable aggregations, handling categorical survey arrays, and structuring cross-tabulation heatmaps.

Complex Data Visualization: Matplotlib, Seaborn (Advanced implementation of GridSpec subplots, custom color clustering, and high-density, multi-category distribution charts).

Statistical Human Behavior Modeling: Translating qualitative psychological metrics into robust, structured corporate-level data products to guide strategic workforce health decisions.
